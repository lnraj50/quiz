Scenario & Demonstration Script

The Situation: The client CMO is tired of 40-slide decks full of clicks. They need a 1-page executive summary connecting ad spend, CRM automations, and server-side tracking to pipeline and revenue.

System Prompt 
You are a Senior MarTech Strategy Partner and Digital Marketing Client Lead.
Your role is to translate granular marketing operations data (ad spend, CTR, conversion rates, CRM lifecycle stages, email automations, data pipeline syncs) into high-level business impact for CMOs, CROs, and CFOs.

Guidelines:
- Never report vanity metrics in isolation (e.g., CTR or Open Rate). Always tether them to commercial reality: Pipeline, CAC, ROAS, LTV, and Revenue.
- Highlight marketing efficiency, automated hours saved, and data hygiene improvements.
- Format for busy C-suite readers: concise, bulleted, actionable, and financially grounded.
User Prompt Template 
Analyze this monthly performance data for our client [Client Name, e.g., Omnichannel eCommerce / B2B SaaS]:

Client Baseline & Target:
- Core Objective: [e.g., Lower blended CAC by 15% and increase qualified SQL-to-Deal conversion]
- Retainer Services Provided: [e.g., Paid Media (Meta/Google), HubSpot CRM automation, Looker BI reporting]

Raw Monthly Performance Numbers:
- Total Ad Spend: [e.g., $65,000 across Meta & Google]
- Blended ROAS / MER: [e.g., 3.4x, down from 3.8x last month]
- Leads / MQLs generated: [e.g., 420 leads at $155 CPL]
- CRM Lead Lifecycle (HubSpot): [e.g., 420 Leads -> 65 SQLs -> 18 Closed Won Deals ($180k Pipeline)]
- Automation & Lifecycle (Email/SMS): [e.g., Abandoned cart/re-engagement automation drove 24% of total revenue]
- Technical/BI Note: [e.g., GA4 and BigQuery export pipeline was updated to include server-side tracking, recovering 12% lost attribution data]

Generate:
1. Executive Summary (3 bullets for the CMO): What went well, what dipped, and why.
2. The "Dollars & Cents" Impact: Translate the server-side tracking and CRM automations into tangible business value/revenue safeguarded.
3. Root Cause & Next Month's Pivot: A non-defensive explanation of why blended ROAS dipped and the proactive corrective action we are implementing.
4. Two Upsell/Expansion Seeds: Identify two logical MarTech roadmap recommendations (e.g., lead scoring model, CDP integration) for next quarter.

Scenario & Demonstration Script

The Situation: Client emails requesting 3 new landing pages, custom GA4 conversion events, and TikTok ad setups by Thursday on an existing $10k/mo retainer.

System Prompt 
You are a Commercial Director at an elite Digital Marketing & MarTech Consultancy.
You are an expert in retainer management, SOW scope protection, and converting client scope creep into billable project change orders or retainer upgrades.
Tone: Warm, consultative, collaborative, yet firm on agency boundaries. Never sound bureaucratic or dismissive.
User Prompt Template 
A client sent this request, which exceeds our contracted monthly retainer scope. Help me respond professionally and position a paid add-on.

Client: [Client Name]
Current Retainer ($12k/mo SOW): [Includes: Managing Meta/Google campaigns up to $50k spend, maintenance of current HubSpot workflows, 1 bi-weekly Looker report].
Out-of-Scope Client Request:
"[Paste email: 'Hey team, we're launching a flash promo next week. Can you guys build 3 new landing pages with Unbounce, hook up custom conversion events in GA4, and set up TikTok ad campaigns by Thursday?']"

Internal Constraints:
- Engineering & Design bandwidth is tight.
- This represents roughly 25-30 hours of unscheduled work.

Generate:
1. Scope Analysis: A clear internal breakdown of what is in-scope vs. out-of-scope.
2. Client Response Email:
   - Validate their flash promo and express excitement.
   - Clarify current bandwidth and what we CAN deliver within the existing retainer (e.g., repurpose 1 existing page).
   - Offer a structured "Fast-Track SOW Add-on" ($3,500 one-off or +$2,500/mo retainer tier) to deliver the full scope without sacrificing their core campaign performance.

3 Agency & MarTech AI Guardrails

Essential operational rules to protect agency liability and client trust.

Risk / Common Trap The Professional MarTech Best Practice
Pasting Client PII into Public LLMs Strictly anonymize data: "Client A generated 120 SQLs" instead of pasting raw corporate directories, lead phone numbers, or email CSVs.
Vague "Algorithm Blame" During Dips Never let AI generate generic excuses. Feed the model exact metrics (frequency, CPM, page load latency) so the response is mathematically grounded.
Over-Promising Technical Timelines AI creates code snippets quickly, but CRM sandbox testing and webhook validation take human hours. Confirm internal bandwidth before sharing estimates.
